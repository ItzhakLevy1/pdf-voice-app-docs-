1. About the App

ReadAloud: Text to Speech is a text-to-speech and text-extraction tool. It lets you:

  • Import text from files, photos, or typed input.
  • Extract readable text from web pages.
  • Convert text into speech using your device's built-in TTS engine.
  • Save, edit, and share extracted text.

The App runs entirely on your device. It does not require an account, does not send your content to our servers, and does not use artificial intelligence models trained on your data. Translation and speech recognition (Tourist Mode) are processed entirely on-device using your device's native capabilities and Google ML Kit.
