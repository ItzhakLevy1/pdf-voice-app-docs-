2. What Data We Do Not Collect

The App is designed to run locally on your Device. The Company does not collect, store, transmit, or have access to any of the following:

  • The text you type, paste, or import.
  • The files, PDFs, DOCX files, images, or documents you open.
  • The text extracted by OCR, PDF parsing, or any other method.
  • The audio produced by the text-to-speech engine.
  • Your voice, microphone input, or any audio recordings.
  • Your contacts, calendar, messages, or call logs.
  • Your precise location.
  • Your browsing history inside the App's WebView.
  • Your saved books, extracted text, or any content you create in the App.

All of the above stays on your Device and is never sent to the Company.

3. What Data We May Collect

The only data we may collect is:

Anonymous crash reports and diagnostics

If the App crashes, we may receive an anonymous crash log containing the stack trace, App version, operating system version, device model, and an anonymous installation identifier. These logs do not contain your User Content, your text, your files, or any personal information.

Anonymous usage statistics

We may collect aggregated information about which features are used and how often. This data is aggregated across all users, cannot be traced back to any individual, and is used solely to prioritise improvements.

You can disable optional data collection at any time in the App's Settings menu.

4. How We Collect Data

6. Web Reader and Third-Party Content

When you use the Web Reader feature to load a website, the page is loaded inside a WebView on your Device. The website you access may independently collect data about you, including your IP address, cookies, device information, and browsing behaviour within that website.

We do not control, receive, or have access to any of this data. The website's own privacy policy and terms of service govern its collection and use.

We strongly recommend that you:

  • Review the privacy policy of any website you access.
  • Avoid entering personal, financial, or sensitive information into websites accessed through the Web Reader.
  • Use the Web Reader only for reading publicly available content.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The App itself does not use cookies. Cookies are a web technology and are not applicable to native mobile applications in the same way they are to websites.

However, when you use the Web Reader feature, the website you load may set cookies inside the WebView. These cookies are stored in the WebView's local storage, are not accessible to the Company, and can be cleared by clearing the App's cache in your Device settings, or by uninstalling the App.

We do not use any analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or tracking technologies in the App.

8. Data Storage and Retention

Data stored locally on your Device

The App stores the following data locally on your Device:

  • Saved books (extracted text).
  • App settings (theme, font size, language, voice preferences, OCR language).
  • Favourites and custom URLs for the Web Reader.
  • Temporary cache files.

This data is stored using the Device's own storage mechanisms. The Company has no access to this data.

Data stored on Company servers

The Company does not store any of your User Content or personal data on its servers. The only data we retain is anonymous crash logs and usage statistics, retained for a maximum of 24 months and not linked to your identity.

Deletion of local data

You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the App, using the "Clear data" option in your Device's app settings, or deleting individual saved books within the App.
